# Third-party provenance and distribution boundary

The original source files in this starter are supplied under its MIT license. The project name and license are initial choices for this bootstrap, not claims of an established upstream project.

## Dependencies

`package.json` declares PptxGenJS 4.0.0 as a runtime dependency. It is not bundled in the starter archive. Installing it downloads that package and its dependencies under their respective licenses. Preserve upstream notices when redistributing dependencies or producing a bundled application. The project license does not replace dependency licenses.

## Referenced projects

Astra Slide, Impeccable, presentation-skill, Knowledge Cat, slides-ai-plugin, PPTKit, PPTKit Presentation, Presenton, pptx-automizer and PPTAgent are referenced as potential integration/donor projects. None of their repository source, templates, images, or full skill prompts is included here. See the two plans for primary sources and `config/upstreams.json` for acquisition metadata.

The starter's six smoke structures are original simple fixtures. They are not copies of Astra's templates. A later adaptation must identify exact source files/commits, applicable licenses/permissions and retained notices. Unresolved rights are not resolved by wrapping a component in an adapter.

## Company assets and fonts

No Ferroque brand material, client data, font binaries or company master template is supplied. The smoke renderer requests Liberation Sans from the local environment. Install fonts separately under their own terms; no font files are redistributed in this archive.

## Publication checklist

Before publishing future changes: inventory copied/adapted files and assets; retain required attribution/license notices; inspect the staged file list; exclude private data and credentials; review package dependencies and their licenses; verify that the chosen project license covers only content the project can license.
